## 2. Specific Technical Corrections Verified in Edition 9.0

1. **The square of `partner(t, g)`.** The map $\mathrm{partner}(t, g) = \mathrm{cconj} + g + \mathrm{topPl}(t)$ has inverse $\mathrm{partner}(-t, \cdot)$. Applying $\mathrm{partner}(t, \cdot)$ twice translates $H$ by $2t$. If $2t \ne 0$, its square fixes no point. The case $E_0 \times \mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z$ with $t = 1$ is checked by `partner_shift_bijection` and `partner_not_involution_Z4` in `lean/HypercubeAndLPTheorems.lean`, and by test 31 in `verification/verify_blueprint.jl`.
2. **The continuous minimum of $x/m + m^2$.** Clear $m > 0$ with $x = 2t^3$ to get $2t^3 + m^3 - 3t^2 m = (m - t)^2(m + 2t) \ge 0$. Equality gives the value $3(x/2)^{2/3}$ at $m = (x/2)^{1/3}$. Local indexing replaces $m^2$ by $m$. The difference for $x/m + m$ at $x = t^2$ reduces to $(m - t)^2 \ge 0$. The formal statement is `exact_envelope_gap_identities`.
3. **The local loss.** In `(Loss)`, $\mathrm{LoP}$ can be positive. The statement of `(Loss)` is $\mathrm{ASK}(u_0) - \mathrm{Ga}(u_0) \le \mathrm{LoP}(C)$. The triple $(4, 3, 1)$ violates $\mathrm{ASK} \le \mathrm{Ga}$, as recorded by `loss_bounds_strict_witness`.
4. **Names and namespaces.** There are `26` live declarations with the short name `adm2`. The height-two `ltLog` in `CMGoodModels/Statement.lean:35` shares a name with a height-one series. The owners of `MAIN`, `Data.Main` and `SizeData₂.MAIN` distinguish three quantities. The basename `Statement.lean` occurs in `86` archive files.
