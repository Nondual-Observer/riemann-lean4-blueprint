# The Riemann Hypothesis on One Page

**Edition 9.0 · Proof outline** · [Hexagon preprint 2611.01031v1](https://hexagonmath.org/abs/2611.01031v1) ([PDF](https://hexagonmath.org/pdf/2611.01031v1)) · [Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24459320](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24459320)

**Theorem (Riemann Hypothesis and Dirichlet $L$-Functions).** *For every integer $N \ge 1$ and every Dirichlet character $\chi \bmod N$, one has $L(s, \chi) \ne 0$ throughout $\Re s > 1/2$ (for $\chi \ne 1$ or $s \ne 1$), and every zero of $L(s, \chi)$ in $0 < \Re s < 1$ satisfies $\Re s = 1/2$. In particular, every non-trivial zero of $\zeta(s)$ lies on the critical line $\Re s = 1/2$.* *[Lean: `Main.lean:4–12`, `Statements.lean:3–16`]*

**1. A zero would violate a window bound.** Fix a finite family $\mathcal F = \{(a_i, \chi_i)\}$ with $a_i > 0$. Omit principal characters of modulus $N_i > 1$. Smooth the prime powers by $R_\chi(y) = \sum_{n \le y} \Lambda(n)\chi(n)(y/n - 1)$, center by $G_1(y) = R_1(y) - y\log y + (1+\gamma)y$ or $G_\chi(y) = R_\chi(y) + y(L'/L)(1,\chi)$, and put $G_{\mathcal F}(y) = \sum_i a_i \Re G_{\chi_i}(y)$. The Mellin transform $\Phi(s) = \int_1^\infty G_{\mathcal F}(y)y^{-s-1}\,dy$ is regular at $s = 1$. At a zero $\rho$ of $L(s,\chi_i)$ its residue is $-\sum_i a_i\{m_i(\rho)+m_i(\bar\rho)\}/[2\rho(\rho-1)]$. Positive weights $a_i > 0$ prevent cancellation and give a positive residue at a real zero $\rho \in (0,1)$. Under $u = \log y$, the gamma density $\frac{\sigma^{k+1}}{k!}u^k e^{-\sigma u}\,du$ concentrates the integral for $D_k(z) = (-1)^k\Phi^{(k)}(z)/k!$ on $[U, (1+\delta)U]$ near $k/\sigma$. Suppose $G_{\mathcal F}(y) \le C y^\theta$ holds on arbitrarily distant windows $[Z, Z^{3/2}]$ ($\delta = 1/2$). A pole from any $\chi_i \in \mathcal F$ in $\Re s > \theta$ then contradicts the derivative bound. In the real branch use $\Re D_k(\sigma)$. In the shifted branch isolate a nearest pole at distance $< \sigma - \theta$ from $\sigma + iv$ and compare $|D_k(\sigma + iv)|$ with the real derivative. Taking $\theta \downarrow 1/2$ for a fixed family containing $\psi$ excludes all zeros to the right. The primitive functional equation, with reflection $s \leftrightarrow 1 - s$, and the finite Euler factors exclude $0 < \Re s < 1/2$. *[Lean: `CharFamily.lean:10–45`, `landauWindow.lean:20–45, 83–154`, `ZeroFreeOfRpowWindow.lean:11`, `ClaimStrip.lean:7–75`]*

**2. Select the target characters.** On each window $[Z, Z^{3/2}]$, one pair $(q, d)$ works throughout ($q \equiv 3 \bmod 8$, $d \equiv 7 \bmod 16$, $p \mid d+1$ for odd primes $p \le u$). The real cyclotomic field and the character field together have degree $n=c\,2^e$ with $1\le c\le\operatorname{ord}(\psi)$ and $n \asymp x^{2+8\varepsilon}$. The CM field $E$ has degree $[E:\mathbb Q] = 4n$ and group $G = E_0 \times H$, with $E_0 = (\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^2$ and $|H| = n$. Conjugation $\mathrm{cconj} = ((1,1),0)$ and translation by $t \in H$ send $G \setminus \Phi$ (where $\Phi = \{g_1 = 0\}$) onto $\Phi$ by $\mathrm{partner}(t,g) = \mathrm{cconj} + g + ((0,0), t)$, with inverse $\mathrm{partner}(-t, \cdot)$. Orthogonality gives $\mathrm{ASK} - \mathrm{MAIN} = \mathrm{De}$. In $\mathrm{ASK}$, each ramp $(A/\ell^e - w)_+ \log\ell$ has multiplicity two at odd powers of inert primes in $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-d})$ outside $\{2,q,d\}$. Each term of the separate sum at $d$ carries $\log d$. The dual sum $\mathrm{De}$ uses $w \cdot \mathrm{GPsi}(\xi, A/w)$. Averaging over $A_0 \le H$ selects the characters in $\Psi_A$ from $\widehat G$. Bounding $\Psi_A \setminus K_p$ separately reduces $G_{\mathcal F}(x) \le x^{1/2 + (e_D+e_L+7)\varepsilon}$ throughout $[Z, Z^{3/2}]$ to a bound on $\mathrm{gap}_A(u_0) = \sum (\mathrm{ASK} - \Re\,\mathrm{MAIN})$. *[Lean: `ExistsPageN.lean:27–49`, `ChBalance.lean:200`, `ChCore.lean:56–75, 283–343`, `SigALe.lean:38`]*

**3. Why odd powers occur.** The supply for $\mathrm{ASK}$ uses $m \asymp x^{1/2-\varepsilon/16}$ signatures $(k+j-1, m-j)$ ($1 \le j \le m$, $k \asymp x^{9/2+10\varepsilon}$) with $m-1$ transitions, ending at $(r, 0)$, $r = k+m-1$. At a good prime $\ell \ge 5$ inert in $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-d})$, supersingular reduction gives height $2$. The comparison series is the Lubin–Tate logarithm $\mathrm{ltLog}(X) = \sum_{j \ge 0} X^{\ell^{2j}}/\ell^j$. In its inverse $\exp_{\mathrm{LT}}(Z) = \sum_{r \ge 1} c_r Z^r = Z - Z^{\ell^2}/\ell + \cdots$, the possible degrees are one modulo $\ell^2 - 1$. The $\ell$-adic denominator exponent at degree $r$ is $\le (r-1)/(\ell^2 - 1)$. Legendre gives $(r - s_\ell(r))/(\ell - 1)$ for the $\ell$-adic valuation of $r!$. The $r!$-normalized jets therefore satisfy
$$
v_\ell(r!\, c_r) \ge \left(\frac{1}{\ell - 1} - \frac{1}{\ell^2 - 1}\right)r - (\lfloor\log_\ell r\rfloor + 1) = \gamma_\ell\, r - (\lfloor\log_\ell r\rfloor + 1),\qquad \gamma_\ell = \frac{\ell}{\ell^2 - 1} = \sum_{\substack{e \ge 1 \\ e\ \mathrm{odd}}} \ell^{-e}.
$$
At an ordinary split prime the height is $1$ and the two rates give $\frac{1}{\ell-1} - \frac{1}{\ell-1} = 0$. Truncation by $\alpha - \min(\alpha, c) = \max(\alpha - c, 0)$ gives the ramps in $\mathrm{ASK}$. *[Lean: `CMGoodModels/Statement.lean:35–49`, `GaussJetBounds/Statement.lean:98–107`, `FormalGroupSystem.lean:548`, `AdmissibleAtGeneratorPlaces.lean:214`]*

**4. Compare slopes at fixed $u_0$.** For the lattices $M_u$, $N_u$ and $P_{u_0}$, use the same positive rank weights throughout. Write $\bar\mu(u_0) = \mathrm{wmean}_d(\mu(N_u) - h(u))$. The four inequalities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
(\mathrm{Loss})\ \mathrm{ASK}(u_0) - \mathrm{Ga}(u_0) \le \mathrm{LoP},\quad
&(\mathrm{Gain})\ \mu(M_u) + \mathrm{Ga}(u_0) + \mathrm{Fo}(u_0) - \mathrm{Ee}(u_0, u) - \mathrm{Lo}_5 \le \mu(N_u),\\
(\mathrm{Slope})\ \bar\mu(u_0) - \log|W_{u_0}| - \mathrm{Err}_{\mathrm{HN}} \le \mu(P_{u_0}),\quad
&(\mathrm{Div})\ \mu(P_{u_0}) + h(u) - \mathrm{Fo}(u_0) \le S_{\rm div} - \sum_t a_t\log T(u_0,u) + \mathrm{Err}_{\rm Div}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $S_{\rm div}=\sum_t m[\phi(a_t)-a_t\log\Omega]$, where $\phi(a)=a\log a-a$. Average $(\mathrm{Gain})$ and $(\mathrm{Div})$ with those weights. The inequality $(\mathrm{Slope})$ eliminates the middle slope. At $(r, 0)$, $\pm\mathrm{Fo}(u_0)$ and $\pm\mu(P_{u_0})$ cancel. Counting bounds $\log T$ from below. Its leading term balances the leading part of $\mathrm{Ee}(u_0,u)$. The period formula $\log\Omega_{\mathrm{val}} = \gamma + \log(2\pi/\mathrm{rd}(E)) - \Re(L'/L)(1,\chi_{-d})$ cancels $\log(\pi/v)$ and gives $\mathrm{Data.Main}(u_0) = \sum_{t,j} \Re\,\mathrm{MAIN}$. Subtract $\mathrm{Data.Main}(u_0)$ to bound $\mathrm{gap}_A(u_0)$. *[Lean: `QuotientSlopeBoundsWith.lean:19–34`, `StatementFrameF.lean:26`, `MeanBoundSecondForm.lean:107–155`, `MeanLogPeriodChowlaSelberg.lean:58–84`, `ChTop.lean:11–46`]*

**5. Optimize the exponents.** Sequential losses along $1 \le j \le m$ cost $\sum_{j=1}^m j \asymp m^2$. For continuous positive $m$, the $x^{2/3}$ barrier is $\min_m(x/m + m^2) = 3(x/2)^{2/3}$ at $m = (x/2)^{1/3}$, of order $x^{2/3}$. The actual $m$ blocks $F.\mathrm{L}_{j,t}$ share the budget $O(f m b x^\varepsilon)$ with $f=2n$, changing the cost from $m^2$ to $m$. Write $m = x^a$, $k = x^\kappa$, $n = x^\nu$. The constraints are $4a \le \nu$ ($m^4 \le n$) and $5a + \nu \le \kappa$ ($m^5 f L \ll k$ for injection into $2^{k-2}$ words). The minimum of $\max(1-a,\ 1+a-\kappa,\ a,\ \kappa-2\nu)$ is $\theta_*(\nu) = \max\bigl(\tfrac{1}{2},\ 1 - \tfrac{\nu}{4},\ \tfrac{2-2\nu}{3},\ \tfrac{5-\nu}{6}\bigr)$. At $\nu_* = 2$ only $(a, \kappa) = (1/2, 9/2)$ attains $\theta_* = 1/2$. This gives $G_{\mathcal F}(x) \le x^{1/2 + O(\varepsilon)}$ and closes the window argument. *[Lean: `DefFam`, `Formalism.L2.DIV.DefFam.L`, `RowLat.lean:202–216`, `LiftLat.lean:147–153`, `DataArith.lean:200`, `MPowFiveMulFMulLLe.lean:9–40`, `CoreGm.lean:12–74`, `TopPlug.lean:12–18`]*
